## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are you collecting my data?

No! Your personal data is never sent to us.

### Why haven't airmiles appeared in my account after a purchase?

Please make sure that you aren't blocking cookies! These are important to ensure that airmiles and the retailer can link the purchase to your account. This won't resolve missing airmiles for existing purchases, but you can attempt to claim via [the missing airmiles section](https://www.avios.com/en-GB/collect-avios/shopping/my-account/missing-avios/).

If that doesn't solve your issue for future purchases, offer more suggestions on the [help page](https://www.avios.com/en-GB/help).
