**Why spend £4 on a folded piece of cardboard?**

Forgot a birthday? Need to send a note? Don't rely on the postman.

_Skip The Stamp_ is the ultimate life-hack. We curate the cheapest, weirdest, and most random items on Amazon. Why? So you can use Amazon's "Gift Message" feature to get a note to your friend tomorrow, guaranteed.

A scourer with "Happy Birthday" attached is funnier (and faster) than a card.

<Image alt="A photo of scourers, with a gift note from the sender" height={1024} src="/apps/skip-the-stamp/promo.png" width={541}/>

## How does it work?

**The item doesn't matter. The speed does.** 

1. **Pick a "Mule":** Browse our list of cheap, silly, or utterly random items (from rubber ducks to spatulas).

2. **Go to Amazon:** Click buy to add the "mule" to your basket.

3. **The Important Bit:** Tick "This is a gift" at checkout. This unlocks the text box.

4. **Write Your Note:** Type your birthday wishes (or insults) into the gift message box.

5. **Done:** Amazon Prime delivers your message on the back of a potato peeler in 24 hours. Beat that, Royal Mail.

So, stop licking envelopes and start browsing. Your friend probably needs a new scouring pad anyway.
