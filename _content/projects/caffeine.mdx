## Keep your screen awake when it matters most

**Caffeine** is a lightweight Chrome and Firefox extension for moments when your screen really should not go to sleep. Its standout feature is **Android support**, giving mobile Firefox users a simple way to keep the screen awake directly from the browser. Whether you're following a recipe, reading documentation, watching a long video, presenting something important, or waiting on a download, Caffeine keeps your display active without forcing you to constantly nudge the mouse.

Instead of changing your whole system setup permanently, Caffeine gives you fast, deliberate control right from the browser. Turn it on when you need it, turn it off when you don't, and let the browser handle the rest.

## Why use it?

- **Keep the display awake on demand** so pages stay visible while you work, read, or watch.
- **A strong Android story** for Firefox users who want this behaviour on mobile, not just desktop.
- **Choose the level of control** with display-only or full system-awake behaviour.
- **Set a timer** when you only need a temporary keep-awake session.
- **Avoid interrupted downloads** by keeping your machine awake until the job is done.
- **Works on Desktop and Android**, so the same idea travels with you.

## Built for simple, practical control

Caffeine is designed to stay out of the way until you need it. One click enables keep-awake mode, another click releases control back to your normal power settings. No clutter, no complicated setup, just a straightforward fix for an annoying problem.
